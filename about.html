About Patrick

A unique combination of hypnosis and counselling

I offer a unique solution which combines clinical hypnosis and counselling to help clients permanently build better and more fulfilling lives, using the tools they develop in therapy.

I am registered with the main UK therapy governing authorities and adopt and work within their codes of ethical practice. I am fully insured and hold a PVG certificate to work with children and adults. I have worked with hundreds of clients during my career to support them in bringing about positive and lasting change in their lives.

I am a fully qualified clinical hypnotherapist and counsellor who uses clinical hypnosis and counselling to help clients achieve lasting change.

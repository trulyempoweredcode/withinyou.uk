Stop Smoking or Vaping

A proven, natural way to quit for good

Hypnotherapy may not be the first method that comes to mind when thinking about smoking cessation, but it is proven to be an effective approach in helping individuals kick the habit.

In simple terms, hypnotherapy is a therapeutic technique that uses guided relaxation, intense concentration, and focused attention to achieve a heightened state of awareness. Your mind is then more open to suggestion and more receptive to changes in behaviour, for example quitting smoking or vaping.

How It Works

How hypnotherapy helps you quit

Guided relaxation

A therapeutic technique using guided relaxation, intense concentration and focused attention to reach a heightened state of awareness.

Reaching the subconscious

Hypnotherapy targets the subconscious mind, where smoking habits and cravings are deeply rooted.

Reframe and quit for good

Your hypnotherapist helps you reframe your thoughts and beliefs about smoking, break the psychological dependence, and quit for good.

But how effective is hypnotherapy for smoking cessation? While individual results may vary, normally depending on client commitment, studies have shown that hypnotherapy is an extremely effective method to help you quit smoking or vaping. In fact, research suggests that hypnotherapy may be more effective than other popular methods, such as nicotine replacement therapy or sheer willpower, in helping individuals quit smoking long-term.
